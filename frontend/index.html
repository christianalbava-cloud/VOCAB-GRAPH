Semantic Connections
A semantic connection is a link drawn between two words because they share meaning, context, or domain — not spelling or sound. When you add a word, the AI measures how closely it relates to every existing node and draws a link where similarity crosses a threshold.
Strong link score ≥ 0.75 — concepts share the same domain or role
Phrase link node is a phrase built from those words
Weak link score < 0.75 — loosely related, same neighborhood
Semantic Similarity — score 0 → 1
The similarity score measures how much two words mean the same thing in the same context. Two words can score high without being synonyms — they just live in the same conceptual neighborhood.
